A COI tracking tool ends up holding more than certificates. It has your subcontractors' policy numbers and endorsements, your project list, your owners' insurance exhibits and, if you connect it, links into your project and accounting systems. That's worth a security review before you sign, and these 12 questions cover what to ask.

They pair with our [COI tracking software demo questions](/blog/coi-tracking-software-demo-questions/) and the [full comparison of COI tracking software for construction](/blog/best-coi-tracking-software-construction/).

## Independent assurance

1. **Which SOC report do you have, and for what period?** The [AICPA](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services) describes System and Organization Controls (SOC) as a suite of services CPAs provide on a service organization's controls. Ask for the report itself, its type, the period it covers, and any exceptions the auditor noted. A logo on a website isn't the report.
2. **Which of your subprocessors are in scope?** Cloud hosting, OCR and AI providers handle your files too.

## Where your data lives and who sees it

3. **Where are documents stored, and in what country?**
4. **Who at your company can open our files, and is that access logged?**
5. **How is access controlled on our side?** Single sign-on, user roles by project, and removing a departing employee's access.
6. **What can brokers and subcontractors see?** They should see their own documents and requests, not your other trades.

## AI and your documents

7. **Which AI models read our documents, and who runs them?**
8. **Are our documents used to train or improve models?** Get the answer in the contract.
9. **If a third-party AI provider processes our files, what are they allowed to keep, and for how long?**

## Integrations

10. **What access does each integration need?** A Procore or accounting connection should have the narrowest access that does the job.

## When you leave

11. **What do we get back, and in what format?** Every document, requirement, result and decision.
12. **How long after we cancel is our data kept, and how is it deleted?**

## Questions for your own team

- Who signs off on the security review, and on what timeline?
- Does your contract with the vendor cover data breach notice and responsibility?
- Do your owners' contracts say anything about where project data can live?
