# Is U.S. construction spending down? What the September 2026 numbers show

> Total spending fell in September and is down for the year. The drop is almost all residential. Nonresidential work, where most commercial contractors live, is still growing.

Published Nov 3, 2026 by Forsured. Market outlook. Source: https://forsured.ai/blog/construction-spending-september-2026/

![-1.9%: Change in U.S. construction spending from August to September 2026](https://forsured.ai/blog/construction-spending-september-2026/hero.png)

## The short version

- Yes. U.S. construction spending ran at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of $2,186.1 billion in September 2026, down 1.9% from August and 2.7% from a year earlier, the Census Bureau reported Nov 2.
- Spending for the first nine months of 2026 was $1,649.6 billion, 2.9% below the same period in 2025.
- Residential drove the drop: private residential spending fell 5.5% in the month.
- Nonresidential kept growing. Private nonresidential rose 1.1% from August, and total nonresidential was 1.8% above September 2025.

Yes. U.S. construction spending fell 1.9% in September 2026, to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of $2,186.1 billion, the Census Bureau reported Monday. That's 2.7% below September 2025. For the first nine months of the year, spending totaled $1,649.6 billion, down 2.9% from the same stretch of 2025.

The headline hides a split, though. Almost all of the decline is residential.

## Where the drop came from

| Segment | September 2026 (annual rate) | vs. August | vs. Sept. 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total | $2,186.1B | -1.9% | -2.7% |
| Total residential | $861.2B | -5.4% | -8.8% |
| Private nonresidential | $790.6B | +1.1% | +2.3% |
| Total nonresidential | $1,325.0B | +0.6% | +1.8% |
| Public | $546.8B | -0.1% | +1.0% |

Private residential alone fell 5.5% in the month, to $848.8 billion. Nonresidential, the work most commercial GCs and trade contractors depend on, is the part still growing.

On the public side, spending was essentially flat. Educational spending was $113.1 billion, virtually unchanged, and highway spending was $149.2 billion, down 0.3%.

These are preliminary numbers. Census revises each month, and August was revised in this release. The next update is due Dec 1.

## Common questions

### How much did U.S. construction spending fall in September 2026?

1.9% from August, to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of $2,186.1 billion, according to the Census Bureau's Nov 2 release. That's 2.7% below September 2025. These are preliminary estimates and can be revised.

### Is nonresidential construction spending still growing?

Yes. Private nonresidential spending was $790.6 billion in September, up 1.1% from August. Total nonresidential spending, private and public, was $1,325.0 billion, 1.8% above a year earlier.

### What about public construction?

Public spending was essentially flat at $546.8 billion, down 0.1% from August and up 1.0% from September 2025. Highway spending was $149.2 billion.
