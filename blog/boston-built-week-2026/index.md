## What is Boston Built Week?

Boston Built Week is a city-wide week of events for the built world, running Nov 15 to 21, 2026. [Boston Built Week](https://bostonbuiltweek.com/) is presented by [Suffolk Technologies](https://suffolktech.com/), and the site describes it as decentralized: dozens of companies host their own sessions, breakfasts, tours and happy hours, from Roxbury to the Seaport to downtown to Kendall Square.

That makes Boston Built Week different from a conference. There's no badge and no main hall. You pick the events that fit, register with each host, and move around the city between them.

## What are the anchor events at Boston Built Week?

The anchor events at Boston Built Week are the four biggest stops on the calendar, and they set the shape of the week:

- **Monday, Nov 16:** the HP Built Leadership Forum (sold out, waitlist only), then Builders & Backers in Cambridge (invite only).
- **Wednesday, Nov 18:** BOOST 7 Demo Day, where startups from the Suffolk Technologies accelerator pitch at Suffolk's headquarters in Roxbury, followed by an after-party.
- **Thursday, Nov 19:** [BuiltWorlds 2026 Venture East: AEC Emerging Tech](https://builtworlds.com/event/2026-aec-emerging-tech/) at Convene, One Boston Place, 201 Washington St, Boston, MA 02108, home of BuiltWorlds Demo Day.

The rest of the week fills in around those anchor events.

## What else is on the Boston Built Week calendar?

The Boston Built Week calendar lists 26 events in all, the four anchor events plus 22 partner events. Beyond the anchor events, Tuesday through Thursday run from breakfast to after-hours, and the calendar has something nearly every hour. You'll find investor AMAs and founder breakfasts, sessions on construction data and AI, a GTM workshop for construction tech teams, a passive house building tour, a commercial real estate awards night at Fenway Park, and plenty of happy hours.

Times overlap, and several stops sit across the river in Cambridge, so plan the day from the calendar before you head out.

## How do you get into Boston Built Week events?

Boston Built Week events each have their own door. Most are free, but most free events need host approval, and some share the address only once you're approved. A few charge, so check the price on each host's page. Register early for anything on the calendar you can't miss.

For getting around, South Station is the hub. The [MBTA](https://www.mbta.com/) Silver Line goes to the Seaport, the Fairmount Line to Newmarket near Suffolk's Roxbury headquarters, and the Red Line to Cambridge. Downtown stops near State station, including BuiltWorlds Demo Day, are a short walk.
