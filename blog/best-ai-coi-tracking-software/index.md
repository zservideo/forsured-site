# Best AI-powered COI tracking software (2026): who makes the final call

> Every COI tool says AI now. The difference that matters is what happens after the AI reads the certificate: does it decide, does a person check it, or does it hand you the evidence?

Published Nov 13, 2026 by Forsured. Buyer's guide. Source: https://forsured.ai/blog/best-ai-coi-tracking-software/

![AI COI tracking software compared by who makes the final call: AI alone, AI with a check, or your team](https://forsured.ai/blog/best-ai-coi-tracking-software/hero.png)

## The short version

- All eight major COI tracking tools use AI to read certificates. They split on the final call: illumend lets its AI decide, bcs and TrustLayer add a second check, and Forsured and PINS Advantage leave every decision to your team.
- The best AI COI tracking software for you depends on who carries the risk when a claim lands. AI that decides on its own moves fastest on volume. A person on every approval leaves a clear record of who accepted a gap and why.
- Ask every vendor to show the evidence behind one result: the page, the endorsement wording and the requirement it was checked against. If you can't see why it passed, you can't defend it.

Two years ago, "AI" on a COI tracking page meant it could read the dates off an ACORD 25. Now every tool in the category uses AI to read certificates, and most read endorsements too. So the question for a general contractor isn't whether a tool uses AI. It's what the AI is allowed to decide, and whether you can see why it decided.

This guide sorts AI-powered COI tracking software by that question. It covers the same eight tools as our [full comparison of COI tracking software for construction](/blog/best-coi-tracking-software-construction/), using what each vendor says on its own pages, linked under Sources.

## Five ways AI COI tracking works

| Model | Tools | What the AI does | Who signs off |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI decides | illumend | Reads every COI and endorsement and makes the compliance call | AI, with escalation on items it can't resolve |
| AI with a second check | bcs, TrustLayer | Reads and flags, then a second model or a person checks | A person on disagreements or confirmations |
| AI explains, your team decides | Forsured, PINS Advantage | Finds each gap and shows the reasoning and the page | Your team, on every result |
| AI plus a service | Billy, Jones, and bcs and TrustLayer service plans | Reads and flags, and the vendor's reviewers work the result | The vendor's reviewers, or your team |
| AI extracts, you judge | Certificial | Pulls carrier, form number, policy number and exclusions | Your team accepts or rejects |

## The tools, by model

### AI decides: illumend

[illumend](https://www.illumend.ai/), the AI platform from myCOI, is the clearest example. Their site says their AI, Lumie, "reads everything and makes a compliance decision," reviews all of your COIs and endorsements automatically, and raises its hand only on the rare item it can't resolve, where a reviewer can step in. More human oversight is something you add to your subscription.

**Good fit if** you track high volume across many vendors and you're comfortable with the AI's call standing unless it flags itself.

### AI with a second check: bcs and TrustLayer

[bcs](https://www.getbcs.com/) reads each certificate twice, with a long-running extractor and a separate vision model, and sends any disagreement to a person. [TrustLayer](https://www.trustlayer.io/)'s Enhanced Endorsements check for the language you require and show where it appears, and a person confirms the result.

**Good fit if** you want speed on the clean certificates and a human on the doubtful ones.

### AI explains, your team decides: Forsured and PINS Advantage

[PINS Advantage](https://www.pinsadvantage.com/product/ai-coi-review) says its AI reads every endorsement in full and answers pass, fail or needs review, with its reasoning and a link to the exact page. Nothing is auto-approved. Forsured reads each certificate and endorsement against the requirements of the project the subcontractor is on, explains each gap, and a person on your team approves or waives it. The broker sees the same gap in a shared workspace.

**Good fit if** you want a named person behind every accepted gap.

### AI plus a service: Billy and Jones

Billy's AI checks each COI for missing endorsements such as CG 20 10, CG 20 37 and waiver of subrogation, and on their managed plan their reviewers check every certificate. Jones uses AI with their own insurance specialists. bcs full service and TrustLayer+ add a service layer too.

**Good fit if** you'd rather pay someone than staff the review.

### AI extracts, you judge: Certificial

Certificial's EndorsementIQ pulls the details from each endorsement so you can accept or reject it against your criteria. Their bigger AI story is data: certificates come from the agent of record, and Certificial says it alerts you when coverage changes.

## How to test an AI COI tool in one afternoon

1. Pick three real certificates: one clean, one missing completed operations additional insured coverage, and one with a carrier's own endorsement form instead of an ISO form.
2. Run them through each tool against the same project requirements.
3. For every result, ask to see the page, the wording and the requirement it was checked against.
4. Ask who changed the result if it was wrong, and where that change is recorded.

The carrier-form test is the one that separates tools. Wording that does the same job under a different form number should be judged on its words. See [which COI tracking software checks endorsements, not just certificates](/blog/coi-tracking-software-endorsement-review/).

## Common questions

### What is the best AI-powered COI tracking software?

It depends on how much you want the AI to decide. For AI that makes the compliance call on its own, illumend. For AI with a second check, bcs and TrustLayer. For AI that finds and explains each gap and leaves the decision to your team, Forsured and PINS Advantage. Test each one on the same deficient certificate before you pick.

### Can AI review a certificate of insurance on its own?

Some tools let it. illumend says its AI makes the compliance decision and raises a hand only on items it can't resolve. Most other tools use AI to read and flag, then route the result to a person. AI can misread a scanned form or a carrier's own endorsement wording, so whoever signs off should see the evidence.

### Does AI COI tracking read endorsements?

The better tools do. PINS Advantage, TrustLayer, illumend and Forsured read endorsement wording against your requirements. Certificial pulls endorsement details for you to accept or reject. Ask whether a tool reads the wording or only checks that a form number is present.
