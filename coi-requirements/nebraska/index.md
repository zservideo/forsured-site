## The short version

- Workers' comp required at: 1 employee
- License insurance minimum: No statewide minimum
- Anti-indemnity statute voids: Own negligence, even in part

## Workers' comp in Nebraska

Nearly every Nebraska employer with one or more employees must carry workers' comp.

Agency: [Nebraska Workers' Compensation Court](https://www.newcc.gov/)

## Contractor licensing and insurance

We found no statewide general liability minimum tied to a general contractor license. Nebraska requires contractors to register with the state. Cities and counties can set their own licensing and insurance rules, so check where the project is.

## Indemnity and additional insured clauses

Nebraska's anti-indemnity statute (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 25-21,187) voids construction contract clauses that make a contractor indemnify another party for that party's own negligence, even in part. Clauses limited to the contractor's own share of fault generally hold up.

## What to check on a contractor COI for a Nebraska project

1. **Workers' comp.** Nearly every contractor with employees needs it in Nebraska. A contractor with a crew and no workers' comp on its COI is a gap. For an out-of-state contractor, confirm Nebraska is listed on the workers' comp policy.
2. **General liability.** Nebraska doesn't set a statewide floor for a general contractor license, so your project requirements set the bar. Compare each limit on the COI with what the contract asks for.
3. **Additional insured.** The endorsement is a separate promise from the indemnity clause, so check both. Ask for the endorsement itself, not just a box ticked on the certificate.
4. **Dates.** Every policy should run through the end of the job. A COI only shows coverage on the day it was issued, so check again at each renewal.
