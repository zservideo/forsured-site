## Workers' comp in West Virginia

All West Virginia employers are required by law to carry workers' comp. The Offices of the Insurance Commissioner reviews applications for exemption.

Agency: [West Virginia Offices of the Insurance Commissioner](https://www.wvinsurance.gov/Employer-Coverage)

## Contractor licensing and insurance

We found no statewide general liability minimum tied to a general contractor license. Cities and counties can set their own licensing and insurance rules, so check where the project is.

## Indemnity and additional insured clauses

West Virginia's anti-indemnity statute (W. Va. Code § 55-8-14) voids construction contract clauses that make a contractor indemnify another party for that party's sole negligence. Clauses that cover shared fault are generally allowed.

## What to check on a contractor COI for a West Virginia project

1. **Workers' comp.** Nearly every contractor with employees needs it in West Virginia. A contractor with a crew and no workers' comp on its COI is a gap. For an out-of-state contractor, confirm West Virginia is listed on the workers' comp policy.
2. **General liability.** West Virginia doesn't set a statewide floor for a general contractor license, so your project requirements set the bar. Compare each limit on the COI with what the contract asks for.
3. **Additional insured.** The endorsement is a separate promise from the indemnity clause, so check both. Ask for the endorsement itself, not just a box ticked on the certificate.
4. **Dates.** Every policy should run through the end of the job. A COI only shows coverage on the day it was issued, so check again at each renewal.
