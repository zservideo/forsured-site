# Georgia contractor insurance requirements

> What Georgia law says about workers' comp, contractor license insurance and indemnity clauses, and what to check on a contractor COI for a project there.

## The short version

- Workers' comp required at: 3 employees
- License insurance minimum: $500K GC; $300K residential
- Anti-indemnity statute voids: Sole negligence

## Workers' comp in Georgia

Georgia businesses with three or more workers, counting regular part-time workers, must carry workers' comp.

Agency: [Georgia State Board of Workers' Compensation](https://sbwc.georgia.gov/frequently-asked-questions/workers-compensation-law-faqs)

## Contractor licensing and insurance

Georgia's licensing board requires general liability of at least $500,000 for general contractors and residential-light commercial contractors and $300,000 for residential-basic contractors, plus proof of workers' comp as state law requires.

## Indemnity and additional insured clauses

Georgia's anti-indemnity statute (Ga. Code Ann. § 13-8-2(b)) voids construction contract clauses that make a contractor indemnify another party for that party's sole negligence. Clauses that cover shared fault are generally allowed.

## What to check on a contractor COI for a Georgia project

1. **Workers' comp.** A contractor below Georgia's threshold can legally go without it, but your project requirements can still require it. Decide what you'll accept, and get an exemption or a policy in writing. For an out-of-state contractor, confirm Georgia is listed on the workers' comp policy.
2. **General liability.** A Georgia license only shows the contractor met the state's floor. Compare each limit on the COI with your project requirements, which are usually higher.
3. **Additional insured.** The endorsement is a separate promise from the indemnity clause, so check both. Ask for the endorsement itself, not just a box ticked on the certificate.
4. **Dates.** Every policy should run through the end of the job. A COI only shows coverage on the day it was issued, so check again at each renewal.
