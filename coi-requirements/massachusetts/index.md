# Massachusetts contractor insurance requirements

> What Massachusetts law says about workers' comp, contractor license insurance and indemnity clauses, and what to check on a contractor COI for a project there.

## The short version

- Workers' comp required at: 1 employee
- License insurance minimum: No statewide minimum
- Anti-indemnity statute voids: Losses the contractor didn't cause

## Workers' comp in Massachusetts

Every Massachusetts employer must carry workers' comp for its employees, whatever the number of employees or hours worked. Corporate officers who own at least 25% of the company can file an affidavit of exemption for themselves.

Agency: [Massachusetts Department of Industrial Accidents](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/department-of-industrial-accidents)

## Contractor licensing and insurance

We found no statewide general liability minimum tied to a general contractor license. Cities and counties can set their own licensing and insurance rules, so check where the project is.

## Indemnity and additional insured clauses

Massachusetts General Laws ch. 149, § 29C voids construction clauses that make a contractor indemnify another party for injury or damage that the contractor, its employees or the companies working under it did not cause.

## What to check on a contractor COI for a Massachusetts project

1. **Workers' comp.** Nearly every contractor with employees needs it in Massachusetts. A contractor with a crew and no workers' comp on its COI is a gap. For an out-of-state contractor, confirm Massachusetts is listed on the workers' comp policy.
2. **General liability.** Massachusetts doesn't set a statewide floor for a general contractor license, so your project requirements set the bar. Compare each limit on the COI with what the contract asks for.
3. **Additional insured.** The endorsement is a separate promise from the indemnity clause, so check both. Ask for the endorsement itself, not just a box ticked on the certificate.
4. **Dates.** Every policy should run through the end of the job. A COI only shows coverage on the day it was issued, so check again at each renewal.
