COI requirements by state

Contractor insurance requirements by state

Every state sets its own rules for workers' comp, contractor licensing and indemnity clauses. Here's what each one says, with the sources linked, and what it means for the contractor COIs on your projects.

By the Forsured teamReviewed Oct 28, 2026

Workers' comp is where states differ most. Most require it from the first employee, some start at three, four or five, construction often has its own lower line, and four states only allow coverage from a state fund.

License insurance minimums are a floor for getting licensed, not a standard for your project. Many states set none, and the ones that do set them well below what most project requirements ask for.

Anti-indemnity statutes limit how much of another party's negligence a contract can push onto a contractor. In a handful of states they also limit additional insured requirements.

These guides are general information, not legal advice. Confirm with the state agency, your broker or your attorney before you rely on them.
